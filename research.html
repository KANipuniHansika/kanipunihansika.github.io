Featured · Singapore Management University

MOTUS

A creativity-support system that uses stylus-based motor training, feedback informed by expert strokes, and drawing prompts to help people develop skills for digital expression.

Creativity support Stylus interaction User studies
My contribution

I co-developed the interactive prototype, implemented real-time stroke capture with a Wacom Movink 13, and contributed to formative and controlled studies of motor learning and creative expression.
